# Band Inventory & Equipment Tracking
[Description]

## <b>Project Overview</b>

### Application Name: [Name]
**This project is a <b>database-driven web application</b>

### Problem to Solve:
A problem with band directors or anyone in charge of equipment may experience difficulties keeping track of all equipment. For example, one student may have checked out multiple instruments or may be using multiple pieces of equipment. Another scenario is that there are separate parts to one uniform (i.e., jacket, pants, and cape) which are assigned numbers (such as 101, 178, 222, etc.), but one student may have a jacket from one uniform and pants from another. Having one place to be able to track everything would be very convenient for the person in charge.

### Intended Users:
Band directors, staff, or student leaders who need to review inventory to add new equipment or disregard old equipment, and see who has what equipment.

### Planned Features:
Track inventory of instruments, equipment, uniforms, and folders <br>
Track the conditions of inventory (i.e., Excellent, Great, Good, Poor) <br>
Track the qualities of [maybe just instruments and some equipment] (i.e., Professional, Intermediate, Beginner) <br>
